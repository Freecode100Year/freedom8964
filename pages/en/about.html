About

freedom8964.com: a directory for collecting, organising and archiving information about June Fourth.

What this site is

This site does not produce content. It only collects, organises, records and archives: material about June Fourth held by archives, human-rights groups, news organisations and video platforms is arranged into directories, and every record points to its original source. For press reports and videos only titles and links are collected; no text or footage is copied. Copyright remains with the original authors and publishers.

Principles

1. Every record has a source. Declassified archives, published works, named witnesses and major news organisations are preferred. Anonymous “insider” claims are not used.

2. Disagreements side by side. Where sources disagree (for example on the death toll), each figure is listed with its source, without choosing between them.

3. Respect for victims and families. No graphic images; the full list of victims is not reproduced — readers are directed to the original source.

4. Copyright. Links to official channels only; nothing re-uploaded or embedded.

5. No commentary, no calls to action.

Corrections

Errors and broken links can be reported with a verifiable source; corrections are dated.

Privacy and security

This is a static site: no cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts, fonts or players, and no personal data collected. Your language choice is remembered only in your own browser. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The site is served through Cloudflare, which, like any network provider, processes requests in transit; this site itself does not record or analyse them.

If you are in a heavily censored region, take care when viewing this material; the Tor Browser may help. People facing digital attacks or transnational repression can seek free help from the Access Now Digital Security Helpline and Citizen Lab.

Preservation

Pages are submitted to the Internet Archive Wayback Machine every week.
